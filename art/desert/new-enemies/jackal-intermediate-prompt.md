## Polecenie dla modelu

Use case: identity-preserve. Create ONE production turnaround sheet for the exact existing Emberfall desert jackal in the attached reference. The reference is the geometry and color model. The goal is four NEW intermediate angles to insert between these existing sprites and make the turn feel continuous. One 2-by-2 asset sheet, landscape if possible, four isolated whole animals with generous clear margins and a genuinely transparent background. No letters, dividing lines, scenery, ground, ground shadows, or vignette.

All four animals walk toward the RIGHT half of their cell in the same physically neutral standing pose. Fixed game camera about 22 degrees above ground; only yaw changes. Top left is 22.5 degrees toward us from right-facing exact side profile. The body is still almost as long as the top-left reference, with the narrow front face and far eye only just appearing. Top right is 67.5 degrees toward us from right-facing side profile, or 22.5 degrees off the top-right front view of the reference. Mostly frontal with a small visible right-pointing snout and a sliver of flank to its left. Bottom left is 22.5 degrees away from us from right-facing side profile. Remains almost as broad as the profile, shows a small amount of the back of the head and rump with nose still visible on the right, between the original top-left and bottom-left angles. Bottom right is 22.5 degrees off direct rear view, between the reference's straight rear and rear three-quarter looking right. Mostly his rear, a small rightward tilt of the axis, a narrow glimpse of the side of the neck, face nearly hidden. DO NOT repeat any reference view or mirror the row. It is critical the top right is much more frontal than the top left and bottom right much more rear-on than bottom left.

Match his actual body: long narrow warm gray-brown wedge muzzle, pointed black nose, angular amber eyes, large alert narrow ears, long lean jointed legs and small distinct feet, low hips, bushy down-hanging tapered tail, dark charcoal grizzled saddle from nape to root of tail, rust brown flanks, cream jaw and belly, purple wine-colored flat collar with the same tiny antique brass clasp visible from front. Same head to body ratios, same camera elevation, same ear height and thickness, same texture realism, same brushed painterly 2D fantasy in-game asset treatment and light from upper left. Preserve the recognizable character exactly. Same measured physical size in every view to interpolate between the existing sprites, with natural foreshortening as it turns. Neutral pose, four anatomically correct standing legs attributable to a body, no speed distortion, no stylization shift. Pure alpha around every cutout. At least 30 pixels gutter between cells and image edge; include all ear tips, feet and tail.
