> Use case: stylized-concept. High-end painted 2D enemy sprites for Emberfall, a grounded isometric dark-fantasy RPG. Create a NEW DUNE JACKAL character, one animal in a coherent eight-facing turnaround, using five essential views and a sixth side reference. Wide 3-column by 2-row sheet on actual transparency, six completely isolated whole figures centered in equal rectangular cells with generous padding. Top-left rightward profile, top-middle facing down-right toward camera (front three-quarter), top-right square-on facing camera. Bottom-left facing up-right away from camera (back three-quarter), bottom-middle looking straight away, bottom-right leftward profile. Same scale, same silhouette proportions, same markings at every angle. Camera is orthographic and about 27 degrees above ground, moderate elevation with readable silhouette and legs. Neutral rigging stance: four paws planted, legs slightly apart and staggered where necessary to see every paw, naturally bent canine hocks, shoulders and thighs visibly embedded in the body. Brushed strands and tufts with graphic clarity like a premium hand-painted game sprite, restrained matte colors, clear sharp opaque edges, broad light from upper left, detailed sculptural anatomy, grounded wildlife illustration, no outline, shadow, floor, motion blur, lighting effects or dust. No text, borders, labels, annotations or dividing lines. Limbs must be convincingly attached; avoid short legs, floating feet and oversized cartoon ears.
